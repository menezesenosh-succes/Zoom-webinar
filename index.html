FAQs

Questions enterprise teams ask us most.

Meetings: everyone can speak and share. Webinars: attendees join view-only and interact through Q&A, chat and polls.

In a meeting, everyone is a participant with camera, mic and screen-share rights. In a webinar, attendees join view-only and interact through Q&A, chat and polls, while only hosts and panellists can present. For anything public-facing, a webinar licence is the right control model.
